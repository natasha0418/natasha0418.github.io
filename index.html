Hello! My name is Natasha Gonsalves and I am a fourth-year undergraduate student at Manipal Institute of Technology where I study Electronics and Communication Engineering.

I’ve always been drawn to building and problem-solving - assembling systems, understanding how they work, and making things function efficiently. It began as a fascination with robotics during my early years and has evolved into a deeper interest in electronic system design, where similar principles of abstraction and optimization are applied at the scale of transistors and circuits. My first experience was at Project MANAS, my university’s autonomous robotics team, where I worked on microcontrollers, communications, and PCB design. I serve as the Technical Head of IE-EnC, the student chapter affiliated with the Institute of Engineers (India), where we hold tech talks with industry experts, organise workshops and manage the operations of the club. This summer, I had interned at IIT Roorkee under Prof Avirup Dasgupta, who heads the Device Research Lab. This internship challenged my basics and gave me the opportunity to work on a cutting-edge device, Nanosheet FETs. Outside of work, I enjoy evenings with a good book and a hot cup of tea.
